Cookie Policy

Last updated:

In short

Non-essential cookies (advertising and analytics) are off by default. They load only after you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you click “Reject”, they never run.

Storage we use

  • gl_lang cookie: remembers the language you chose. It lasts 1 year and holds only a language code.
  • Local storage: keeps your acceptance of the terms and your cookie choice. It is not sent to our server.

What needs your consent

  • Advertising: cookies set by ad networks are governed by the respective network’s privacy policy.
  • Analytics: used for aggregate figures such as visit counts and device types, to improve the site.

Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer to change your decision at any time. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. For more detail see the Privacy Policy.
